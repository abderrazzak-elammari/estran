Laboratory data of the conical island experiment (Briggs et al. 1995),
as distributed with the NTHMP benchmark problems, problem 6:
https://github.com/rjleveque/nthmp-benchmark-problems (BP06-FrankG-Solitary_wave_on_a_conical_island).

- `ts2a.txt`, `ts2b.txt`, `ts2cnew1.txt`: surface elevation (m) at gauges 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, 16, 22
  for cases A, B, C, every 0.04 s from t = 20 s on the wavemaker clock.
- `run2a.txt`, `run2b.txt`, `run2c.txt`: maximum vertical run-up (cm) around the island. Angles are
  measured from the -y direction, 90 deg is the lee side, 270 deg faces the wavemaker.

Gauge positions (m): 1-4 at x = 5.76 (A), 6.82 (B), 7.56 (C) and y = 16.05, 14.55, 13.05, 11.55;
6 (9.36, 13.80); 9 (10.36, 13.80); 16 (12.96, 11.22); 22 (15.56, 13.80). Island centre (12.96, 13.80).
